= Writing Architecture Decision Records

An `adr` item records a durable decision made while addressing a change.
The ADR explains context, the decision, alternatives, and consequences.

== Item template

[source,asciidoc]
----
[#ADR-NNN, item, role=adr, status=proposed, title="Short decision title"]
--
Context:: What problem requires a decision?
Decision:: What was decided?
Alternatives:: What was considered and rejected?
Consequences:: What follows from the decision?

leads_to:DES-NNN[]

tracer:links[]
--
----

An accepted ADR leads to at least one stable design concept.
The preset validates this structural condition without enforcing status transitions.
