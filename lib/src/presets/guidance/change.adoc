= Writing Changes

A `change` item records a proposed feature, fix, or improvement.
It links to the durable requirements it addresses and the ADRs that record decisions made for the change.

== Item template

[source,asciidoc]
----
[#CHG-NNN, item, role=change, status=proposed, title="Short change title"]
--
The change describes the outcome to deliver.

addresses:REQ-NNN[]
records:ADR-NNN[]

tracer:links[]
--
----

== Lifecycle

The recommended states are `proposed`, `refined`, `accepted`, `implemented`, `verified`, and `closed`.
A change may also be `rejected`.

A refined change has at least one use case.
An accepted change addresses at least one requirement.
The initial preset validates these structural conditions without enforcing transitions.
