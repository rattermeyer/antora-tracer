## Why

The `requirements-engineering-max` preset includes a `business_goal` role but provides no authoring guidance, leaving users without a consistent way to distinguish a business outcome from a solution or project activity.

## What Changes

- Add business-goal authoring guidance with an item template, writing rules, and review checklist.
- Register that page as the `business_goal` guidance in the `requirements-engineering-max` preset.
